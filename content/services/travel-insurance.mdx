# Why Travel Insurance Matters

Travel often involves uncertainties that may be beyond your control.

A medical emergency in a foreign country, a delayed flight, lost baggage, or a cancelled trip can result in unexpected expenses and inconvenience.

Travel insurance helps provide financial support and assistance during such situations, allowing travellers to manage disruptions more effectively.

Whether travelling for leisure, business, education, or family commitments, travel insurance can play an important role in protecting both your travel plans and finances.

---

# Advantages Of Travel Insurance

### Medical Emergency Coverage

Provides financial protection against eligible medical expenses and hospitalization during travel.

---

### Baggage Protection

Coverage for loss, theft, or delay of checked-in baggage, helping reduce inconvenience during travel.

---

### Passport & Document Protection

Provides assistance in situations involving loss of passports or important travel documents.

---

### Flight Delay & Missed Connection Coverage

Compensation for eligible expenses arising from travel delays, missed connections, or trip interruptions.

---

### Personal Liability Protection

Coverage against certain liabilities that may arise during travel, subject to policy terms and conditions.

---

### Adventure Sports Cover

Optional protection available under select policies for adventure and recreational activities.

---

### Protection Against Theft & Loss

Coverage for specified losses arising from theft, scams, or loss of money during travel, subject to policy terms.

---

### Peace Of Mind

Travel with greater confidence knowing that financial assistance and support may be available during unexpected situations.
