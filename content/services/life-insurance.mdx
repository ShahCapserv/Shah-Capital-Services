# Protection First. Planning Next.

Before wealth creation comes wealth protection.

At Shah Capital Services, we believe life insurance is one of the most important foundations of financial planning. Before building an investment portfolio, planning for retirement, or pursuing long-term financial goals, it is essential to ensure that your family's financial future is protected against life's uncertainties.

# Why Life Insurance Matters

Life insurance is designed to protect the people who depend on you financially.

Life insurance can help provide:
- Family income protection
- Financial security for dependents
- Protection against outstanding liabilities
- Support for children's education goals
- Estate and succession planning support
- Long-term financial continuity

# A Common Misconception

Many people believe investing and wealth creation should come before insurance planning.

In reality, protection planning should come first.

Life insurance is not an investment decision.

It is a risk management decision.

# Are You Under-Insured Or Over-Insured?

A structured life insurance review helps assess:
- Existing insurance coverage
- Income replacement requirements
- Outstanding loans and liabilities
- Family responsibilities
- Future financial goals
- Current policy suitability
